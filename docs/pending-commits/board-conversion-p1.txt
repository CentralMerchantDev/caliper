P1.2 -- public/board.js's createBoard() wraps world-registry.js's own
bucketed reservation store, no second spatial index. whereIs(id) is O(1);
inCells(i,j,w,d,k) translates a cell rectangle to metres and calls the
registry's allOverlapping.

P1.3 -- canPlace checks exactly two things: SPACE (a free foot+clear box,
levels tall) and GROUND (every foot cell's kind is in standsOn, where kind
is land-use.js's classifyAt at k=0, or the surface of whatever piece sits
directly below at k>0 -- Mark's stacking rule, a rooftop bench standing on
the roof below it). Watched red twice, for two different real bugs, not
narrated as smooth: (1) the pre-rewrite terrain check used
world-registry.js's WATER/ROCK constants, which answer "is this volume
entirely submerged/buried", not "can a piece stand here" -- an unbounded
yMax could never read as buried, and a piece reaching into open air above
underwater ground is never "submerged". Superseded by the current
standsOn/classifyAt design once the 3D spec landed. (2) after the rewrite,
the stacking test itself failed: a rooftop piece above a 2-level building
on flat ground at y=10 (deliberately not y=0) read as floating in mid-air
with nothing beneath it. Diagnosis: k's vertical extent was computed from
absolute sea level, not local ground height -- invisible at y=0, wrong
everywhere else. Fixed by threading a real groundY (sampled via heightAt)
through every vertical-extent calculation. Watched green after, same
assertions, unedited.

P1.4 -- place/remove/replace/move, each reversible. test/board.test.ts:
place then remove is byte-identical (JSON.stringify equality on
board.list()) to before; place then replace then remove is identical; a
refused move or replace leaves the board completely untouched, not
partially applied.

P1.5 -- determinism, and a real infrastructure problem found and fixed
along the way. Building the world even once at module scope inside
test/run.mjs's shared process (~110 other bundled test files' own
world-builds already resident) reliably crashed the whole suite with a V8
out-of-memory error ("Committing semi space failed"), watched three times
with different exit codes, across three escalating mitigation attempts.
The actual fix: every generateWorld() call for this phase now happens in
scripts/_board-adapter-probe.mjs, run as a genuinely separate node child
process (execFileSync), which builds the world, runs every P1.1 check
itself, and reports a small JSON summary (never the full world, never the
full piece list twice) back over stdout. Two builds, same seed, same
16,935 pieces, same ids in the same order, same SHA-256 digest.
