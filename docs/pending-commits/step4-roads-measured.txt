World rebalance, step 4: the road network measured, not re-planned

WORLD-DENSITY-FINDINGS.md §5: "there is no junction list, no adjacency, no
connected-component check anywhere in the plan... a road that reaches
nothing looks exactly like one that does." Mark's own words: "random roads
that are not connected, have no interconnectivity." This is the
measurement that finds out whether that is still true, built from the
axis-aligned spans generateWorld() already returns -- two roads of
different axes meet if each one's fixed coordinate falls inside the
other's span, which is the whole geometric content of a junction the plan
never states. Every road is a node; two that cross are adjacent;
connected components over that graph answer the question directly.

scripts/measure-roads.mjs, committed so the numbers are re-takeable:

    world: 1357 roads
    junctions: 4511 crossing pairs
    connected components: 52
    largest component: 1053 roads (77.6% of the network)
    stranded (cross nothing): 38 roads (2.8%) -- 15 AVENUE, 10 BOULEVARD,
        7 RAMP, 5 STREET, 1 FREEWAY
    18 settlements have their OWN roads split across more than one
        component (freeway/approach/coast-road/link -- the regional
        connectors -- split worst, across 8-11 components each; several
        ordinary settlements, e.g. coastal-2, coastal-7, split across 3-4)

THE FINDING: Mark's complaint is real but not total. Most of the network
(77.6%) genuinely is one connected whole -- not "random, disconnected
roads" everywhere -- but nearly a quarter of it (22.4%, roughly 300 roads
across 51 separate components) sits outside that one component, and 38
roads connect to literally nothing. The regional connectors (freeway,
approach, coast-road, link) are the most fragmented category by component
count, which matters more than an ordinary settlement's internal split:
they exist specifically to join settlements together, so a freeway broken
into 11 pieces is not delivering the one thing a freeway is for. 18
settlements (a third of all 55) have their own local streets split
internally, meaning within a single town the street grid alone does not
guarantee you can drive from one part to another.

NOT STARTED, PER THE BRIEF: no road was added, removed, or moved to
produce these numbers, and none should be inferred from them. Re-planning
the network is explicitly out of scope for this pass.

How it is known to work: node scripts/measure-roads.mjs, run against the
current committed world. No source file changed; this is a new,
independent measurement script only.
