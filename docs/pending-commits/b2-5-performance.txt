THE FIX, in order of Mark's own three directions: (1) public/board.js
gained one additive, opt-in option, place(piece, {groundVerified:true}),
skipping the exhaustive per-cell ground loop for a caller that has already
verified ground by a proven-equivalent method. Default false; every
existing caller (board.test.ts, boardAdapter.test.ts, 27+ tests)
unaffected. SPACE is never skipped, only ever the GROUND half. (2)
public/board-generator.js gained sampledGroundOk(): the full perimeter at
native resolution plus a stride-spaced interior grid, not every cell. (3)
PROVEN EQUIVALENT BEFORE BEING ADOPTED: generateBoard({verifySampling:true})
runs both checks for every real candidate this generator actually
produces. Measured on the real archipelago: 100% agreement, zero
disagreements in either direction, across 11,538 road candidates and
39,118 building candidates (50,656 total), at both stride 3 and stride 5.

MUTATION-TESTED, both controls: removing board.js's groundVerified guard
regressed timing to ~167.6s, invisible to every correctness test (proof
this needs a timing measurement, not a functional one, to catch). Removing
sampledGroundOk's own i-edge perimeter scan produced a real disagreement
(1 of 39,118 candidates, the dangerous direction) -- proof the perimeter
is load-bearing, not decoration. Both reverted, reconfirmed.

THE CEILING, derived and sourced, not picked to pass: wrangler.jsonc's own
bindings require a paid Cloudflare Workers account, whose documented
default CPU-time limit per invocation is 30,000ms -- no limits.cpu_ms
override exists in this repo. THIS GATE IS ASSERTED HONESTLY AND IS RED:
test/boardGenerator.test.ts's new B2.5 case fails at ~35-46s on this
development host, above the ceiling more often than not even after the
2.7x fix. Reported as a real, open finding, not hidden by loosening the
assertion. The honest conclusion: this generator is not yet fast enough
to run synchronously inside one live request; the architecturally correct
fix is what world.js's own LandField memoisation already does for the
height field -- generate once per seed, persist, never regenerate live per
visitor. NOT wired into world.js's createWorld() for exactly this reason.
