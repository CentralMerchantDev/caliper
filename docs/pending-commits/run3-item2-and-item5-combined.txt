docs/specs/BOARD-REBUILD-PLAN.md's B4: "Roads from roadkit. Buildings
from the kit. Trees from propModel. Props from the manifest." Trees
(propModel's VARIED path) landed in RUN2; this adds the manifest's OTHER

WHAT THIS IS: public/board-render.js's new scatterStreetLamps(), calling
propModel("lampPost", ...) (resolves through props.js's own
`MODELS["lampPost"] = MODELS["lamp-street"]` alias) for one lamp per
40th road piece (bounded, named as deliberately modest, the same pattern
scatterTrees already established for trees).

WHAT THIS IS NOT: the brief's full B4 ask also names roads from roadkit
and building typologies from the kit -- neither is done by this commit.
Investigated both before choosing this slice instead of a rushed attempt
at either:

  - ROADS FROM ROADKIT: board-generator.js's own road pieces are a fixed
    9 m wide (ROAD_WIDTH); roadkit.js's own ROAD_STANDARDS classes are
    LANE (10 m, closest) or STREET (18 m) -- no exact match. Wiring
    roadkit.straight() onto a 9 m piece means either accepting a real
    width mismatch or changing ROAD_WIDTH, a generation-affecting choice
    this run did not have grounds to make unilaterally. Named here, not
    solved.
  - BUILDING TYPOLOGIES FROM THE KIT: public/buildings.js's own building()
    dispatcher sizes each typology INTERNALLY from its own seed/cellW/
    cellD -- some typologies (bldVilla, bldTower, bldWarehouse,
    bldWorkshop) accept a cellW/cellD override, but two (bldHighStreet-
    Terrace, bldBusinessParkBlock) have HARD-CODED footprints with no
    override at all, checked directly in the source, not assumed. A
    naive "pass the piece's own foot as cellW/cellD" wiring would
    silently overhang the plot for those two and possibly others whose
    clamp ranges don't cover a given plot size -- exactly the "17.4% of
    buildings overhung their own plot" defect public/layout.js's own
    typologyFor/fits mechanism was built to prevent (docs comment in
    that file). Building an equivalent fits-safe selection for the NEW
    pipeline, tested and mutation-verified, plus checking the perf impact
    of real per-typology geometry across ~17,600 buildings (unmerged
    LOD0 parts, likely re-triggering the same culling/draw-call
    regression RUN3 item 1 found and gated), is real, separate,
    multi-step work -- not attempted under time pressure tonight.
