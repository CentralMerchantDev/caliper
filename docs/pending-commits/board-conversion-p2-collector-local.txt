BOARD-CONVERSION-PLAN.md P2 finish, item 2: collectors and locals
(P2.3 proper) -- the majority of the network, three real bugs found
converting it

What this is: buildCollectorLocalNetwork() in public/road-network.js
converts the EXISTING generated network's AVENUE (collector) and
STREET/LANE (local) spans -- 1,012 of 1,357 roads, 74.6%, the majority
of the network, not a token slice -- into socket-verified piece chains.
BOULEVARD/FREEWAY/RAMP stay spans this pass, used for crossing detection
only. Full evidence: docs/audits/P2-COLLECTOR-LOCAL.md.

Method: every road is split at every point another road's endpoint
touches it or a perpendicular road crosses it (more general than
scripts/measure-roads.mjs's own crosses(), which misses an
endpoint-to-midspan touch). Every point where 2+ converted legs meet
gets a real junction piece (mixedJunction) -- one socket per leg at
THAT leg's own class/width/lanes, since a real intersection here mixes
classes (AVENUE crossing STREET), unlike the arterial layer's uniform
standardJunction.

Three real bugs, watched red before being called fixed, none assumed
correct on the first attempt:

1. Junction socket position used the wrong bearing -- 12,765 of 21,285
   joins failed on first measurement. A junction places each socket
   AWAY from its own centre; the first version used the leg's own
   INTO-the-node bearing directly, putting every socket 2x its own
   radius from where the piece actually ended. Fixed to match
   buildArterialNetwork's own convention. Failures: 12,765 -> 1,650.

2. The junction-radius formula was sized for arterials, not locals --
   remaining large-magnitude failures (up to 8.5 m) after bug 1's fix.
   standardJunctionRadius() gives 12-16 m for LANE/STREET.
   CITY-PLANNING-SPEC.md Section 1.6 states corner radii directly:
   "urban standard 3.0-4.6 m ... Vehicle-oriented 9.1-22.9 m." Local
   intersections are the urban case; reusing the arterial formula
   clamped short local blocks (61-183 m) hard enough that a junction's
   socket sat metres from the piece's real end. Fixed: a sourced 3.8 m
   for STREET/LANE/ALLEY, the existing formula (unchanged, still
   correct there) for AVENUE/BOULEVARD, applied per LEG rather than per
   node.

3. Two independently-computed versions of the same crossing point could
   differ by close to a millimetre -- 92 junctions, an exactly repeated
   "0.001m apart" failure, comfortably past verifySocketMating's 1e-6
   tolerance even though inside nodeKey's own 1mm grouping bucket.
   Fixed: every leg at a node now trims from that node's ONE shared
   coordinate, not its own independently-computed endpoint.

Gate, measured (node -e "...buildCollectorLocalNetwork(world.roads)...",
also test/collectorLocalNetwork.test.ts): 3,778 junctions total, 2,899
(76.7%) fully verified, 879 not. Every one of the 879 is checked and
explained -- a leg's own sourced corner radius exceeds its adjacent
block's length, a real property of the existing generator's block
spacing (density-scaled avEff/stEff in generateSettlement()), not a
conversion defect -- confirmed 879/879 explained via an independent
re-derivation of the radius rule, not the internal counter trusted
blindly. Gate NOT fully met: 879 named exceptions, not zero, per this
project's own standard (a fix or a conversion earns the right to say
"handled" only when the exception list is checked, not when it is
empty by luck).

Also measured, named not verified: 623 points where a converted
AVENUE collector physically touches an unconverted BOULEVARD/FREEWAY/
RAMP road (collectorFeedsArterialContacts) -- counted, not
socket-verified, since the arterial-tier side has no piece in this
pass. Reconciling the two arterial representations (old spans, new MST
layer) is real, unresolved work already named in P2-ARTERIAL.md.
