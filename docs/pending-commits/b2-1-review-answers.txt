BOARD-ADAPTER.JS DIES WITH city-plan.js, ON A DELAY. board-region.js LIVES.
Ruled on directly, not by category (Mark). board-adapter.js's own header
says "generateWorld() -> a list of placed pieces" and it imports ROADS from
city-plan.js directly -- confirmed by reading it, not assumed. It is
imported live today by world-render-3d.js (boardPiecesById, P4.1's picking
path) -- confirmed the same way, one real import, not the two files a first
guess suggested. Quarantining it before B2 supplies real pieces of its own
would break live selection, so it is quarantined AFTER, not alongside
city-plan.js. board-region.js imports only from board.js, has no generator
dependency, and lives as a board.js utility. Both docs/specs/BOARD-REBUILD-
PLAN.md's "what dies, what lives" and B2.1's own "not yet decided" list
updated to match -- the open item marked resolved rather than left to
contradict the ruling above it.

THE SHARED PRINCIPLE BEHIND BOTH APPROVED B2 MECHANISMS, NAMED IN THE
PROTOCOL: "construct what you want, do not filter for it and hope" --
settlement density decided from LANDMASSES.kind as a table (not a uniform
sprinkle filtered by what survives a buildability check), and roads as an
explicit junction graph (not centrelines with crossings detected
afterward, the exact shape of the project's oldest reported defect).
Recorded next to Failure pattern E as its positive-form counterpart: make
the invariant a constraint on how the data is produced, not a predicate
checked against data produced some other way.
