What changed. `hasGarden` is now options-overridable
(`options.hasGarden !== undefined ? options.hasGarden : r6 > 0.3`),
matching this file's sibling idiom (bldVilla's hasPorch/hasBay/hasDormers/
hasChimney) exactly, and gates a real ground-level garden bed + low hedge
at the building's rear.

Placement, and why the rear. A blind plan review (fresh subagent, no other
context, before any code was written) read the real function and found a
genuine collision risk the first placement idea (along the front) would
have hit: the front margin already carries the stair core(s) --
`stairPosition === "center"` places a 4.0m-wide block at x=0 spanning that
exact band, `"dual"` places two 3.0m blocks at x=+-bW*0.35 -- so a garden
strip along the front would interpenetrate the stairs for roughly 70% of
seeds (`r4 < 0.7`). The rear only carries two thin mirrored window/sill
boxes at x=+-bW*0.28; the bed is centred at x=0 and sized narrower than
that gap (`gardenW = min(bW*0.4, bW*0.28*2 - 1.6)`), and depth-limited to
the existing (footD-bD)/2 rear margin, so it cannot collide with the
stairs (front-only, any stairPosition) or the rear windows (outside the
bed's width). `stairPosition` is not itself options-overridable in this
function (rolled purely from seed), which is why the fix avoids depending
on its value at all rather than trying to steer around it per-case.
