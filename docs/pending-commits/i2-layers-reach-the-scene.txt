I2: layers reach the scene -- the first moment anything built in A-H is

What was wrong: apply-layers.js, instance-groups.js, resolve-models.js and
model-registry.js were each proven correct in isolation across Phases B/C
and none of them ran for real. public/city-render.js fed groupByVariant()
the raw planCity() output directly -- no layer was ever applied, and nothing
pulled an overridden placement out of its shared InstancedMesh group, which
would double-draw it (once in the group, once wherever the override draws
it).

How it's fixed: buildScenePlacements({ instance, world, heightAt }), exported
from city-render.js and used by buildWorld() itself, runs applyLayers
between planCity and instance-groups.js's partitionForInstancing -- pure, no
THREE, so it is the one thing about "layers reach the scene" provable
without a renderer. buildWorld() now feeds groupByVariant only the
`instanced` half and draws `overridden` individually: each is resolved
against resolve-models.js + a fresh model-registry.js instance; a verified
"replace" draws its registered geometry; a bare retint/move draws the
placement's own stock model with the edit applied (move gives an absolute
new x/z; the height is NOT re-assessed at the new position, stated plainly
rather than claimed more exact than it is); an unresolved "replace" (nothing
is registered yet -- that is I5) is refused outright, never a default-shaped
fallback, matching resolve-models.js's own stated discipline.

Test-first, against the real generated plan (~20,000 placements, not a
fixture): test/cityRenderScenePlacements.test.ts asserts a hand-injected
retint layer moves EXACTLY the targeted plot to `overridden`, the total
(instanced.length + overridden.length) is unchanged from before the layer
existed, the overridden plot is genuinely absent from `instanced` (would
double-draw otherwise), and every other placement is still present. A
second test confirms an unedited world leaves everything instanced.
