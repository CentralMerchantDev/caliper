## 3. WHY THE FIXES COME BEFORE TERRAIN

**FIX-2 is not a rename.** `massing` is a shape-segment array of two to four
entries and the value model reads it as a storey count. Mark's ruling is that a
mega-tower is 100+ storeys. At real proportions `sqrt(100)=10` against
`sqrt(4)=2` — the per-unit dilution and the total-worth inversion both move by
five times. **The scale fix and the value model are one fix.**

If the house/condo inversion stops holding at real proportions, **say so and show
the numbers.** Do not tune until it holds — that would be making a check pass by
changing the check.

**FIX-5 and FIX-6 unjam the instruments.** Roughly 140 of 185 mutations target
deleted files. `mutationEvidence.test.ts` is red *because* the manifest is stale,
and `mutate.mjs` refuses while the suite is red — a gate holding itself shut.
Purge first; expect several of the seven failures to clear with it. Then one
shared recorder, per Mark's ruling that both tools work properly or we do not
keep both.
