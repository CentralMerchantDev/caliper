## What was found

**Two real findings, both requiring a change inside `city-render.js`
(CLI-lane-owned), so neither was fixed — see "Why nothing was fixed"
below.**

1. **Street lighting.** `props.js` has a complete, tested, 7-part
   `lamp-street` model (248 LOD0 tris) correctly aliased and reachable;
   `city-render.js` never calls it, building every one of the world's
   2,407 lamp posts (the largest single prop count this lane has found
   fixed-count evidence for this week) from two inline primitives
   instead — the exact bug class `prop-models.js`'s own header names as
   already fixed for `bin`/`bench`/`busShelter`, never closed for lamps.
   One real wrinkle found and stated plainly, not glossed over: the
   richer model's merged geometry has no per-part colour, so a direct
   swap would lose the emissive lamp-head glow that reads as "lit" at
   night, unless the model definition is also extended to expose the
   head separately. **Filed as a cross-lane request**, `docs/CROSS-LANE-
   REQUESTS.md` §2, with the wrinkle stated up front rather than
   presenting a risk-free diff that isn't one.
2. **Trees.** Two hand-built shapes stand in for `prop-models.js`'s own
   12 real species/age variants, across what is very likely the single
   largest prop count in the world (26,000+ candidate positions in the
   wide-landscape pass alone, before filtering). This traces
   `K6-BUILDINGS.md`'s own already-recorded "bright repeated trees"
   verdict to an actual, specific, previously-untraced cause. Unlike the
   lamp post, wiring this in is not decision-free: it means a real
   draw-call/memory multiplier (2 `InstancedMesh` pools per chunk per LOD
   becoming up to 12) on a world whose frame-time budget is already not
   cleanly met at two of three reference cameras. **Filed as a decision
   for Mark**, `docs/DECISIONS-FOR-MARK.md` §3, with three options and a
   recommendation, not attempted.

**Negative results, checked and recorded rather than left silent:**
`bin`/`bench`/`busShelter` are correctly wired (already fixed, prior
work) but share the tree/lamp situation at smaller scale — a named,
reasoned scoping decision in `prop-models.js`'s own header, not a bug,
