# CALIPER — ASSET LANE REPORT: 6-TIER QUALITY HIERARCHY (540 MODELS)

## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents the completion of the 6-tier quality hierarchy across the entire Caliper asset ecosystem in `sandbox-spike-assets` on branch `assets-lane`.

In response to the charter requirements:
- **Matrix**: Exactly 9 asset categories $\times$ 6 quality tiers $\times$ 10 models per tier = **540 procedural models**.
- **Quality Tiers**:
  1. **Showstoppers** (`showstopper`): 10 models/category ($\ge 350$ tris LOD0, hero parametric landmarks).
  2. **Luxury** (`luxury`): 10 models/category ($\ge 220$ tris LOD0, prestigious sculptural architecture & high-end assets).
  3. **High-End** (`highend`): 10 models/category ($\ge 140$ tris LOD0, sleek contemporary designs & enhanced finishes).
  4. **Mid-High** (`midhigh`): 10 models/category ($\ge 80$ tris LOD0, detailed urban fabric).
  5. **Mid** (`mid`): 10 models/category ($\ge 40$ tris LOD0, standard modular municipal & commercial elements).
  6. **Mid-Low** (`midlow`): 10 models/category ($\ge 12$ tris LOD0, utilitarian, minimalist, and lightweight elements).

## 3. Technical & Architectural Conformance

1. **Modular Grid Compliance**:
   - Every model conforms strictly to Caliper's 8m CELL and 4m LEVEL modular framework.
   - Exact bounding limits: $y \in [0, \text{height}]$, $x \in [-w/2, w/2]$, $z \in [-d/2, d/2]$.
2. **Three LODs Per Model**:
   - `LOD0`: Full detail geometric mesh.
   - `LOD1`: Mid-distance simplification (30–70% tri reduction).
   - `LOD2`: Extreme distance bounding proxy ($\le 12$ tris).
3. **Geometry Merging Architecture**:
   - `mergeGeometries` standard converts all indexed Three.js primitives to non-indexed representations before buffer concatenation, preventing vertex distortion or UV displacement across platforms.
4. **Verification & Quality Gate**:
   - `node scratch/validate_all_540.mjs`: **540/540 models passed with 0 bounding violations, 0 inverted LODs, and 0 missing registrations**.
   - `node test/run.mjs`: **645/645 tests passing (100% green)**.
   - `npx tsc --noEmit`: **100% clean, 0 type errors**.
